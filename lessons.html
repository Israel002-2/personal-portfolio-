Getting started

  1. Say hello

    Message me on WhatsApp or email with the student's class and what they find difficult.

  2. Find the gaps

    I check where the student is now, so we start from what they already know.

  3. Learn step by step

    Clear explanations, worked examples and practice, at a pace that builds confidence.

  4. Track progress

    Regular tests and honest feedback show what is improving and what needs revision.
